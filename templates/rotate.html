How it works

  1. Alice calls rotate(newAddress) from her old wallet.
  2. The contract records rotatedTo[msg.sender] = newAddress.
  3. When you paste Alice's new address, KnownHosts reads the registry: approved by the old key → ✅.
  4. An attacker can't write to Alice's entry without Alice's private key → 🔴.
